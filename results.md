# Results

## Data cleaning
- Raw listings: 30,555
- Listings with no price (inactive / unpriced): 8,758 (29%)
- Priced listings after removing outliers (outside $39-$1702/night): 21,385

## 1. Price by borough
- City-wide median nightly price: $176
- Manhattan: median $239 (+36% vs city), 9,532 priced listings
- Brooklyn: median $153 (-13% vs city), 7,184 priced listings
- Queens: median $121 (-31% vs city), 3,579 priced listings
- Bronx: median $108 (-39% vs city), 802 priced listings
- Staten Island: median $108 (-39% vs city), 288 priced listings

## 2. Room-type mix and price
- Entire home/apt: 55% of listings, median $221/night
- Private room: 43% of listings, median $108/night
- Hotel room: 2% of listings, median $432/night
- Shared room: 1% of listings, median $75/night

## 3. Demand (reviews in the last 12 months)
- Only 35% of all listings received a review in the last 12 months
- Manhattan: 34% of listings active, 4.7 reviews per listing
- Brooklyn: 35% of listings active, 4.5 reviews per listing
- Queens: 37% of listings active, 5.0 reviews per listing
- Bronx: 36% of listings active, 3.6 reviews per listing
- Staten Island: 39% of listings active, 3.5 reviews per listing
- Private room: 37% active
- Entire home/apt: 34% active
- Shared room: 32% active
- Hotel room: 22% active

## 4. Impact of short-term rental regulation (Local Law 18)
- 81% of listings require a minimum stay of 30+ nights (i.e. operate as monthly rentals)
- 19% of listings allow stays under 30 nights
- Of short-term listings, 39% show a registration/licence number
- Active in last 12 months: short-term 76% vs 30+ nights 25%
- Median price: short-term $299 vs 30+ nights $143

## 5. Host concentration
- 56% of listings belong to hosts with more than one listing
- 27% of listings belong to hosts with 10+ listings (likely commercial operators)
- Unique hosts: 16,474
